## Source disagreements and unclear points (shown on the About page)

1. **Founding year:** June **1986** (source 1) versus **1987** (source 6). Both are shown.
2. **Number of delegates:** 75 (Juba, source 1) versus 45 (Rumbek main office, sources 9-10). Probably different bodies or periods, to be confirmed.
3. **Revival date** (about December 1997) comes from a single interview.
4. A 2013 report calls the Kampala electorate "thousands of students" yet gives vote counts of about 750. We only state the vote counts.
