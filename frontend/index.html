About RUMYU-UG

The Rumbek Youth Union (RUMYU) is a historic community organisation. Founded in Khartoum in the mid-1980s and revived in Rumbek in the late 1990s, it has grown into a powerful voice for the youth globally.

RUMYU-UG serves as the Uganda chapter, acting as a safe haven and a home away from home for students, professionals, and families living in the diaspora.
