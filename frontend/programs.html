Our Programs & Initiatives

Empowering the youth of Rumbek through culture, education, sports, and community support.

Focus Areas

Building a Stronger Community

Our programs are designed to unite the Agar community in Uganda, preserve our rich heritage, and support the next generation.

Agaar Cultural Day

A dedicated day to gather the Agar community in Kampala to showcase and celebrate Agaar Madong culture. We educate the youth on our manners, character, respect, marriage and dating processes, taboos, habits, and traditional dances.

Education & Mentorship

Prioritizing academic excellence. We provide guidance, mentorship, and resources to students living in Uganda to ensure they navigate their educational journey successfully.

Agaar League & New Year

Fostering unity through sports. We host the Agaar League Tournament, culminating in a massive New Year Celebration at the end of the year and the beginning of the next, bringing everyone together.

Democratic Elections

Ensuring transparent and peaceful transitions of power. Every two years, the union conducts elections to select new leadership, keeping the organization dynamic and accountable to the youth.

Peacebuilding & Unity

Bringing youth together across the three counties and the diaspora to promote non-violent conflict resolution, reconciliation, and lasting unity.
