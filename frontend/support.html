Stand Together for a Better Future

While all Agar in Kampala are inherently part of our community, RUMYU-UG requires resources to run our educational, cultural, and safety programs. We invite individuals, businesses, and diaspora organizations to support our mission.

  • Education Sponsorship: Fund tuition, buy books, or sponsor a student's academic journey.
  • Program Funding: Support workshops, leadership training, and cultural events.
  • Resource Donation: Donate laptops, sports equipment, or educational materials.
  • Mentorship: Offer your professional time to guide our youth.
Transparency: All contributions go directly to community programs. The executive committee will contact you to discuss how your support will be utilized.
